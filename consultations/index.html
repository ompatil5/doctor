What to bring or upload

  • All previous blood reports, including old ones
  • Current medicines or photos of the strips
  • Glucose readings, or CGM or meter downloads
  • Discharge summaries, if you were hospitalised
  • A list of questions

What happens in the first visit

A detailed history, an examination, a review of reports, and a plan in plain language. You leave knowing what we decided and why.

For international patients

Patients from abroad

Please share your time zone and country. I will confirm whether I can safely advise remotely. Time-zone friendly appointment slots can be arranged for follow-up and second opinion reviews.

When to see a doctor

Book an appointment if you need assistance adjusting blood sugar medications, evaluating borderline thyroid labs, or establishing a long-term metabolic health plan. For immediate acute emergencies (e.g. loss of consciousness, persistent severe vomiting, chest pain, or suspected diabetic ketoacidosis), please proceed immediately to the casualty ward of the nearest hospital.
