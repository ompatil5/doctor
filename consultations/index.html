Online Consultations (India & Worldwide)

Online video consultations are available for individuals unable to attend clinic in person. Please ensure recent lab reports (within the last 3 to 6 months) are readily available before the scheduled call.

For International Patients

International patients are welcome. When completing the consultation request form below, please specify your current location and preferred time zone to help schedule a mutually convenient appointment.

What to Bring to Your Consultation

  • Previous laboratory reports, including old ones (HbA1c, fasting/post-prandial blood sugar, lipid panel, kidney function)
  • Current medication prescriptions with exact brand names and daily dosages
  • Home blood glucose logs, Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) summaries, or blood pressure records
  • Thyroid function tests (TSH, Free T3, Free T4) if consulting for thyroid or metabolic management
