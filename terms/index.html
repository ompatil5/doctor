Terms and Conditions

Terms governing the use of this website, online appointment enquiries, educational publications, and clinical communication standards.

Last updated: 2026 • Dr. Firdous Shaikh Medical Practice, Mumbai

1. Acceptance of terms

By accessing or browsing this website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please discontinue using this website.

2. Educational nature of website content

All clinical articles, diagnostic guides, research monographs, book descriptions, and educational commentaries authored by Dr. Firdous Shaikh on this website are provided solely for general educational purposes. They represent evidence-based medical perspectives intended to help patients and caregivers better understand metabolic disorders.

Browsing this website, reading published guides, or submitting a contact form does not establish a formal physician-patient relationship. A clinical relationship is formed only upon mutual agreement during an individual in-person consultation or formal video clinical visit.

3. Appointment booking and enquiries

Submitting an appointment request or clinical enquiry through our website forms is a preliminary scheduling request. Our clinical administrative team reviews requests and confirms available appointment dates, times, and pre-consultation instructions via telephone or secure email. Submission of a form does not constitute guaranteed appointment reservation until formally confirmed.

4. Telemedicine and remote consultations

Online video consultations for individuals residing across India and worldwide are conducted strictly in accordance with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines notified by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and applicable Indian healthcare laws. Patients participating in video visits must provide valid photographic identification and make available previous laboratory records (HbA1c, fasting glucose, thyroid panels, and current medications) for accurate clinical assessment.

5. Emergency care protocol

This website and its digital messaging channels are not monitored for acute medical emergencies. If you or someone in your care experiences severe, acute symptoms (including severe hypoglycaemia, loss of consciousness, chest pain, persistent vomiting, or symptoms of diabetic ketoacidosis), please proceed immediately to the nearest hospital casualty or emergency department, or contact local emergency ambulance services.
