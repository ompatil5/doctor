Women's Health and Diabetes

Careful, empathetic endocrine and metabolic care tailored to the physiological and life stages of women.

Women carry diabetes differently. Hormones shift through puberty, pregnancy and menopause, and social pressure often pushes a woman's own health to the bottom of the list. In India, many women are diagnosed late.

I founded the DAWN Foundation (Diabetes and Women's Network) in October 2025 to change that: screening, education and support built around women. In my clinic, I see women for PCOS, gestational diabetes, thyroid problems around pregnancy, and diabetes in all life stages.

Areas of Clinical Focus

PCOS & Insulin Resistance

Managing hormonal imbalances, weight struggles, and metabolic risks associated with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome through lifestyle and medical support.

Gestational Diabetes

Careful monitoring, nutritional structuring, and safe insulin titration during pregnancy to safeguard both maternal and neonatal health.

Pre-Pregnancy Planning

Optimising HbA1c and thyroid levels before conception to minimise risks of birth anomalies and gestational complications.

Post-Partum Follow-up

Long-term screening and preventive lifestyle guidance for women with a history of gestational diabetes, protecting future metabolic health.

When to see a doctor

Schedule a visit if you have irregular periods accompanied by weight or skin changes, elevated blood sugar first detected during a routine antenatal test, or a family history of diabetes while planning a pregnancy. If pregnant and experiencing sudden severe headaches, visual disturbances, or sudden swelling, contact your obstetric team or visit the nearest maternity hospital immediately.
