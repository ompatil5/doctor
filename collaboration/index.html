Collaboration and Partnerships

Dr. Shaikh and DAWN Foundation welcome collaboration with diabetes organisations, researchers, hospitals, community groups and educators.

An Invitation to Collaborate

DAWN Foundation is seeking partnership with international diabetes organisations that share this commitment to closing gaps in continuity of care and to culturally responsive, evidence-based tools for psychosocial assessment in diabetes. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how our ongoing work in paediatric T1DM engagement, T2DM, chronic disease conditions, and diabetes-distress research might align with and contribute to your organisation's priorities.

Areas of collaboration

  • Paediatric type 1 diabetes continuity of care
  • Cross-cultural validation of the Modified Diabetes Distress Scale
  • Community screening and awareness
  • Professional education

Current partnerships and affiliations

T1International, SOO-DAWI, Liberia Diabetes Alliance, LedBy, Diabetes India Mental Health Group and Green Pencil Foundation.
