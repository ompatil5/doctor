Collaboration and Partnerships

Dr. Firdous Shaikh and DAWN Foundation welcome collaboration with diabetes organisations, researchers, hospitals, community groups and educators.

◆ An Invitation to Collaborate

DAWN Foundation is seeking partnership with international diabetes organisations that share this commitment to closing gaps in continuity of care and to culturally responsive, evidence-based tools for psychosocial assessment in diabetes. We welcome the opportunity to discuss how our ongoing work in paediatric T1DM engagement, T2DM, chronic disease conditions, and diabetes-distress research might align with and contribute to your organisation's priorities.

Areas of Collaboration

Paediatric Type 1 Diabetes

Promoting structured continuity of care, peer support networks, school engagement, and family caregiver education.

Psychosocial Research

Cross-cultural refinement and validation of the Modified Diabetes Distress Scale (mDDS) across global cohorts.

Community Screening

Grassroots detection camps, preventive lipid and glucose screening, and workplace metabolic wellness initiatives.

Professional Education

Clinician workshops, webinars, evidence-based guidelines, consensus papers, and interdisciplinary clinical exchanges.
