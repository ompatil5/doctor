Clinical Heritage & Background

Medical Training & Clinical Journey

Dr. Firdous Shaikh trained in Medicine (MBBS) at Zhejiang University School of Medicine in China. She completed her internship there in 2012 and cleared the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination in India on the very first attempt. She practised in Emergency Medicine as a Chief Medical Officer at Lady Hardinge Medical College in Delhi. Emergency work instils rapid clinical decision-making and clear communication, principles that continue to guide her daily clinical practice.

Diabetes drew Dr. Shaikh's interest because it is a condition where physicians can create progressive, tangible improvements in people living with multiple chronic health conditions. Dr. Shaikh completed Diabetology training at the Asian Heart Institute in Mumbai (BKC), working in the Department of Diabetology and Endocrinology with a focus on perioperative diabetes care. She went on to earn a postgraduate qualification in Clinical Endocrinology and Diabetes from the Royal College of Physicians, UK.

From 2020 to 2022, Dr. Shaikh served as a Consultant Diabetologist at the BKC Dedicated Covid Health Centre in Mumbai (one of the largest Covid centres in Mumbai and across India), managing over 500 patients with diabetes concurrently. She designed and implemented a real-time glucose monitoring chart workflow that allowed the multidisciplinary clinical team to identify immediately which patients required urgent medical attention. Dr. Shaikh was felicitated by the Mayor of Mumbai, Kishori Pednekar, for this frontline service.

Dr. Shaikh sees patients at Jyoti Clinic in Bandra West and at Niramay Diabetes Centre in Mira Road East. She has organised multiple diabetes screening camps for school students, Mumbai police personnel and underserved communities, and has pursued advanced clinical education. In 2024, Dr. Shaikh completed a Certificate Course in Clinical Diabetology and Obesity with Harvard Medical School and an Advanced Clinical Diabetes programme with Emory School of Medicine (USA).

Alongside patient consultations, Dr. Shaikh actively conducts clinical research and medical writing. Her academic work has been presented at ATTD (Advanced Technologies & Treatments for Diabetes). The ATTD Breakthrough Outcomes Award was presented to Dr. Firdous Shaikh in March 2025 in Amsterdam. She has co-authored 12+ research papers on diabetes, obesity, and diabetes and technology, presented at national and international platforms.

What Dr. Shaikh cares about most

The Emotional Side of Diabetes

Diabetes distress is real and under-recognised. Dr. Shaikh co-authored milestone validation research on the Modified Diabetes Distress Scale (mDDS) for the Indian population.

Women & Diabetes

Dr. Shaikh founded the DAWN Foundation (Diabetes and Women's Network) in 2025 to focus on continuity of care in diabetes, obesity and chronic conditions, and mental wellbeing.

Global Advocacy
