Diabetes Care in Mumbai, With Online Consultation Across India and Worldwide

Personalised, unhurried care for type 1, type 2, prediabetes and gestational diabetes.

Consultations are available in Mumbai, across India and around the world through online visits. Learn about consultations →

Diabetes is not one disease and it is not one treatment. A thirty-year-old with type 1, a fifty-five-year-old with long-standing type 2, and a young woman with gestational diabetes need three very different conversations. Dr. Shaikh works to determine which conversation each patient needs.

Type 2 diabetes

Most patients Dr. Shaikh sees have type 2 diabetes, and many have been told the same three things: take the tablet, cut sugar, walk more. Those are helpful steps, but they do not constitute a complete plan. At your first visit, Dr. Shaikh reviews your reports, daily routine and medications, and together you decide what to address first. That may mean adjusting or simplifying medication, introducing newer drug classes where appropriate, or evaluating sleep and stress.

Some people, especially those diagnosed early, can bring their blood sugar into a non-diabetic range with sustained lifestyle changes and appropriate medication support. Dr. Shaikh has written a book on this, Path to Diabetes Mellitus Type 2 Remission. Dr. Shaikh is careful with the term “reversal”, because remission requires ongoing follow-up and is not a permanent cure. Dr. Shaikh discusses openly and honestly whether remission is a realistic goal for each individual.

Type 1 diabetes

Living with type 1 diabetes is a daily responsibility, and modern technology can significantly lighten that load. Dr. Shaikh works with insulin regimens, insulin pumps, flash and continuous glucose monitoring, and carbohydrate counting. Dr. Shaikh has co-authored abstracts on insulin pump therapy and flash glucose monitoring presented at international conferences, and is an active member of ISPAD. Dr. Shaikh also maintains a long-term commitment to practical, accessible care for individuals with type 1 diabetes through work with T1International.

Prediabetes

Prediabetes offers the best window of opportunity for intervention, yet many people receive little practical guidance. Dr. Shaikh reviews your glucose, HbA1c, body weight, liver health and lipid profile together. Dr. Shaikh has presented research on lifestyle interventions for prediabetes, emphasising sustainable, practical adjustments.

Gestational diabetes and pregnancy

Elevated blood glucose during pregnancy can cause anxiety and is often insufficiently explained. Dr. Shaikh supports expectant mothers through timely diagnosis, nutritional guidance, glucose monitoring and, when necessary, insulin therapy. Dr. Shaikh provides continuous follow-up after delivery, as the risk of future diabetes persists beyond pregnancy.

Diabetic foot care

Foot complications are among the most preventable consequences of diabetes. Dr. Shaikh authored Footprints of Wellness: Managing Foot Care in Diabetes, and conducts routine foot examinations at every annual review.

Diabetes and surgery

For planned surgical procedures, glycaemic control before and after surgery is vital. Perioperative diabetes management has formed an integral part of Dr. Shaikh's clinical focus since early residency at the Asian Heart Institute.

Diabetes distress and mental health

Diabetes distress is a recognised medical reality rather than personal fatigue or weakness. Dr. Shaikh screens for diabetes distress and discusses emotional wellbeing openly throughout treatment.

When to see a doctor

Schedule a clinical review if your fasting glucose remains consistently above target, your HbA1c has changed unexpectedly, or you experience recurrent episodes of low blood sugar (shakiness, sweating, confusion). If you experience severe symptoms such as difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting, or unresponsiveness, please go immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.
