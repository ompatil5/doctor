DAWN Foundation (Diabetes and Women's Network)

Screening, education and healthcare support built around women.

The DAWN Foundation (Diabetes and Women's Network) was founded in October 2025 by Dr. Firdous Shaikh.

Why DAWN Was Founded

Women carry diabetes differently. Hormones shift through puberty, pregnancy and menopause, and social pressure often pushes a woman's own health to the bottom of the list. In India, women often come last in their own family's health priorities, and many are diagnosed late.

Dr. Shaikh founded the DAWN Foundation to help change that reality through screening, education and support built specifically around women.

Clinical Focus for Women

In clinical practice, Dr. Shaikh sees women for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), gestational diabetes, thyroid disorders around pregnancy, and diabetes at all stages of life.

For detailed medical information on PCOS, gestational diabetes and thyroid conditions in pregnancy, please visit the Women's Health and Mental Health page.
