Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding clinic visits, treatment approaches, thyroid disorders, and diabetes remission.

Do you see patients online from other countries?

Online consultations for patients living outside India are scheduled with consideration for international time zones. Please note that telemedicine regulations vary by jurisdiction, and video consultations are advisory and educational. TO CONFIRM: remote international consultation policy

What should I bring to my first appointment?

All previous reports, current medicines, and any glucose readings or CGM downloads.

Do you treat both diabetes and thyroid?

Yes. They often occur together and affect each other.

Can type 2 diabetes be reversed?

Some people achieve remission, especially if diagnosed early, but it needs ongoing monitoring. Dr. Shaikh will assess whether it is realistic for you.

Do you treat children with type 1 diabetes?

Please contact the clinic with the child's age and current regimen so Dr. Shaikh can confirm how best to help.

Do you offer second opinions?

Yes, upload your reports before the visit.

Have a question not listed here?

Feel free to reach out directly through the clinic contact form with any questions regarding appointment booking, lab reports, or consultation formats.
