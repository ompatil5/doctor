Clinical Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common clinical questions regarding clinic appointments, diagnostic reports, thyroid disorders, and diabetes remission.

Appointments & Visits Diabetes Care & Remission Thyroid & Combined Care International & Online

◆ Clinic Appointments & Preparation

What should I bring to my first appointment?

All previous reports, current medicines, and any glucose readings or CGM downloads.

Do you offer second opinions?

Yes, upload your reports before the visit.

◆ Diabetes Care, Types & Remission

Can type 2 diabetes be reversed?

Some people achieve remission, especially if diagnosed early, but it needs ongoing monitoring. Dr. Shaikh will assess whether it is realistic for you.

Do you treat children with type 1 diabetes?

Please contact the clinic with the child's age and current regimen so Dr. Shaikh can confirm how best to help.

◆ Thyroid & Combined Metabolic Care

Do you treat both diabetes and thyroid?

Yes. They often occur together and affect each other.

◆ International & Remote Teleconsultations

Do you see patients online from other countries?

Online consultations for patients living outside India are scheduled with consideration for international time zones. Please note that telemedicine regulations vary by jurisdiction, and video consultations are advisory and educational.

Have a question not listed here?

Feel free to reach out directly through the clinic contact form with any queries regarding appointment booking, lab reports, or consultation formats in Mumbai and worldwide.
