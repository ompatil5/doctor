Legal & Data Transparency

Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website is handled, stored, and protected in compliance with medical confidentiality standards.

Last updated: October 2026 • TO CONFIRM: privacy policy legal review and data controller contact email

1. Information we collect

When you use the booking and contact forms on this website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Your preferred consultation type (in-person clinic visit or online video consultation)
  • Clinical enquiry notes, current reports, or health information you voluntarily provide in the message field

2. How we use your information

The information submitted through this website is used solely for:

  • Responding to your clinical enquiry or appointment request
  • Scheduling and coordinating your consultation appointment
  • Sending pre-consultation guidance regarding required lab tests or reports to bring
  • Administrative communication regarding follow-up appointments

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your contact or medical details with third parties for marketing or commercial advertising purposes under any circumstances.

3. Third-party processing and transmission

This website uses secure encrypted HTTPS transmission for all pages. Form submissions may be routed via secure clinical form processing services to deliver messages directly to clinic administrative staff. We ensure service providers adhere to industry data encryption and confidentiality standards.

4. Medical confidentiality and data retention

Health and contact records submitted during consultations are maintained in strict accordance with the ethical regulations of the National Medical Commission (NMC), Maharashtra Medical Council (MMC), and applicable Indian data protection principles. Records are retained only for the duration required for clinical care and statutory record-keeping obligations.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website is designed for privacy. It does not use intrusive behavioural tracking, cross-site trackers, or commercial advertising pixels. If basic aggregate visitor analytics are employed to understand page performance, they collect anonymized, non-personally identifiable technical information.

6. Your rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information you have submitted to us, to request corrections to outdated details, or to request the deletion of non-statutory communication records. To exercise these rights, please contact the clinic.
