Publications & Clinical Guides

Books and Patient Education

Translating complex metabolic research into structured, practical guidance that empowers patients and primary care clinicians.

Living well with diabetes requires clear understanding, consistent self-monitoring skills, and practical daily strategies. Beyond clinical consultations, Dr. Shaikh has authored clinical guides and contributed to professional textbooks to make evidence-based metabolic management accessible to patients, families, and healthcare providers.

Authored books

Path to Diabetes Mellitus Type 2 Remission

2024

Dr. Firdous Shaikh

A comprehensive clinical and lifestyle guide detailing the scientific principles of type 2 diabetes remission. Focuses on structured nutritional protocols, gradual weight reduction, continuous glucose monitoring, and maintaining metabolic stability safely under medical supervision.

Availability: TO CONFIRM: publisher and purchase or download link

Footprints of Wellness: Managing Foot Care in Diabetes

2025

Dr. Firdous Shaikh • Launched at Diabetes India Conference

An essential guide for diabetic neuropathy, peripheral vascular assessment, daily inspection regimens, and ulcer prevention. Diabetic foot complications remain one of the most preventable causes of hospitalisation; early detection and proper daily care save limbs.

Availability: TO CONFIRM: publisher and purchase or download link

Textbook chapters
