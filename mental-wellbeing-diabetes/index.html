Whole-Person Diabetes Care

Mental Health and Wellbeing in Diabetes

Diabetes can affect emotional wellbeing for people of every age and gender. Compassionate, practical psychosocial support is an indispensable pillar of modern metabolic care.

Consultations are available in Mumbai, across India and around the world through online visits. Learn about consultations →

Diabetes Distress & Emotional Wellbeing

Diabetes distress is real, highly prevalent, and frequently under-recognised in standard clinical settings. Living with diabetes requires constant vigilance: checking numbers, calculating meals, managing hypoglycemia anxiety, and navigating chronic exhaustion.

Academic & Clinical Leadership

Dr. Firdous Shaikh co-authored milestone validation research on the Modified Diabetes Distress Scale (mDDS) for the Indian population, has served on the Diabetes India Mental Health Group, and is an active member of the international Psychosocial Aspects of Diabetes (PSAD) study group.

She regularly explores sleep quality, chronic stressors, emotional burnout, and mood fluctuations alongside blood glucose trends during every clinical consultation.

Integrated Clinical Care & Ethical Referrals

Dr. Shaikh considers emotional health alongside glucose monitoring, nutritional rhythm, physical activity, and pharmacological therapies. When distress, severe depression, or clinical anxiety warrant specialized psychological or psychiatric intervention, she coordinates transparent, professional referrals within an interdisciplinary medical team.

Support for Every Person Living with Diabetes

This clinical approach is designed for all patients living with diabetes : men, women, teenagers, and caregivers. Medical consultations do not substitute for emergency psychiatric care, but provide a safe, respectful environment to discuss the emotional toll of chronic disease.
