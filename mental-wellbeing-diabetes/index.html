Mental Health and Wellbeing in Diabetes

Living with diabetes can affect wellbeing for people of every age and gender. This page describes respectful support alongside diabetes care.

Diabetes distress and wellbeing

Diabetes distress is real and under-recognised. Dr. Firdous Shaikh co-authored work on the Modified Diabetes Distress Scale for India, has served on the Diabetes India Mental Health Group, and is a member of the PSAD study group.

She screens for diabetes distress and discusses emotional wellbeing as part of clinical care. When something is outside her area of practice, she discusses referral to an appropriate specialist.
