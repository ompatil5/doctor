Thyroid Care in Mumbai

Careful evaluation of thyroid function, hormone dosage, and symptoms beyond routine blood tests.

Consultations are available in Mumbai, across India and around the world through online visits. Learn about consultations →

Thyroid problems are common, easy to miss, and often over-simplified into “take the tablet for life.” Sometimes that is right. Sometimes the dose is wrong, the timing is wrong, or the diagnosis needs a second look.

Book a thyroid consultation Read: Borderline TSH guide

Types of thyroid conditions

UnderactiveHypothyroidism

Hypothyroidism means the thyroid makes too little hormone. Symptoms can be gradual and assessment combines symptoms with thyroid blood tests.

OveractiveHyperthyroidism

Hyperthyroidism means the thyroid makes too much hormone. It may affect heart rate, temperature tolerance, weight and energy, and needs assessment.

Hashimoto'sAutoimmune

Hashimoto's thyroiditis is an autoimmune condition that can gradually reduce thyroid function. Blood tests and symptoms help guide follow-up.

Graves' diseaseAutoimmune

Graves' disease is an autoimmune cause of an overactive thyroid. It can affect thyroid levels and, for some people, the eyes.

Nodules and goitreNeck swelling

Nodules are lumps within the thyroid; a goitre is an enlarged thyroid. Examination, blood tests and imaging can clarify the cause.

SubclinicalBorderline tests

Subclinical hypothyroidism describes a thyroid blood-test pattern with little or no clear symptom change. Decisions depend on the whole clinical picture.

Thyroid in pregnancyPregnancy

Thyroid function is especially important before and during pregnancy. Existing thyroid conditions may need closer review with the maternity team.

What to bring to your consultation

Please bring all earlier thyroid reports (TSH, free T3, free T4, thyroid antibodies, ultrasound neck), a complete list of current medicines and supplements, and the exact time you take your thyroid tablet in the morning.

Common Questions

Do I need the tablet for life?

Often yes for hypothyroidism, but not always, and not at the same dose forever.

My TSH is slightly high. Do I need treatment?

It depends on your age, symptoms, antibodies and whether you are pregnant or planning to be.

Does thyroid cause weight gain?

It can contribute modestly. It rarely explains large weight gain on its own.

When to see a doctor

Consult a doctor if you notice a visible swelling or lump in your neck, unexplained rapid heart rate, severe heat or cold intolerance, or if you are planning a pregnancy with a history of thyroid illness. For sudden severe breathlessness or difficulty swallowing, seek immediate emergency medical care.
