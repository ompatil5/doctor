Thyroid Care in Mumbai

Careful evaluation of thyroid function, hormone dosage, and symptoms beyond routine blood tests.

Thyroid problems are common, easy to miss, and often over-simplified into “take the tablet for life.” Sometimes that is right. Sometimes the dose is wrong, the timing is wrong, or the diagnosis needs a second look.

Book a thyroid consultation Read: Borderline TSH guide

What I see most often

  • Under-active thyroid (hypothyroidism), including Hashimoto's
  • Over-active thyroid (hyperthyroidism) and Graves' disease
  • Thyroid swellings and nodules
  • Thyroid issues in pregnancy and while planning pregnancy
  • Thyroid and weight, thyroid and fatigue, thyroid and hair fall: what is thyroid-related and what is not

What to bring to your consultation

Please bring all earlier thyroid reports (TSH, free T3, free T4, thyroid antibodies, ultrasound neck), a complete list of current medicines and supplements, and the exact time you take your thyroid tablet in the morning.

Common questions

Do I need the tablet for life?

Often yes for hypothyroidism, but not always, and not at the same dose forever.

My TSH is slightly high. Do I need treatment?

It depends on your age, symptoms, antibodies and whether you are pregnant or planning to be.

Does thyroid cause weight gain?

It can contribute modestly. It rarely explains large weight gain on its own.

When to see a doctor

Consult a doctor if you notice a visible swelling or lump in your neck, unexplained rapid heart rate, severe heat or cold intolerance, or if you are planning a pregnancy with a history of thyroid illness. For sudden severe breathlessness or difficulty swallowing, seek immediate emergency medical care.
