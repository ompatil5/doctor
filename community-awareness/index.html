Outreach & Public Health

Community and Awareness

Taking preventive screening to communities where early detection makes the greatest difference.

1,000+ Citizens Screened
06+ Mumbai Hubs
Asia Book Lipid Record
Mayor COVID-19 Duty

Dr. Shaikh believes screening must reach people in their communities, because many will not visit a clinic until symptoms arise. Over the past several years Dr. Shaikh has conducted diabetes detection camps across Mumbai, in Khar, Bandra, Vile Parle, Kurla, Mira Road and Qureshi Nagar, including camps for the Mumbai police, school students and girls who are survivors of human trafficking in Vasai (along with CPR training).
