Clinical Service

Medical Weight Management in Mumbai

Evidence-based, medical management for obesity and metabolic dysfunction, free of crash diets or unrealistic claims.

Consultations are available in Mumbai, across India and around the world through online visits. Learn about consultations →

Obesity is a medical condition and not a failure of willpower. Many individuals consulting Dr. Shaikh have tried numerous restrictive diets and feel disheartened. Dr. Shaikh's approach begins with identifying why weight has been difficult to manage: hormonal imbalances, medications, sleep quality, chronic stress, insulin resistance, or lifestyle plans that were not sustainable.

Book a consultation What to bring

How care works

  • A proper assessment: weight and waist, HbA1c, lipids, liver and thyroid
  • A nutrition and activity plan built around your food and schedule
  • Medicines, when they are indicated, explained clearly with their benefits and side effects
  • Regular review, because weight management is a long conversation

Related conditions Dr. Shaikh treats

Metabolic and endocrine factors: Fatty liver (NAFLD/NASH), high cholesterol and dyslipidaemia, insulin resistance, prediabetes, and PCOS-related weight issues.

A note on medical ethics

Dr. Shaikh maintains this practice free of before-and-after photographs or promises of rapid weight loss, upholding ethical medical care.

When to see a doctor

Seek a medical evaluation if weight gain is accompanied by elevated blood pressure, rising blood sugar or HbA1c, abnormal liver enzymes, daytime sleepiness (suggestive of sleep apnoea), or menstrual irregularities. If you experience severe chest pain, shortness of breath on exertion, or sudden leg swelling, please visit the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
